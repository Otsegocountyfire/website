About the Authority
Otsego County Fire Authority

Our Mission

The Otsego County Fire Authority provides coordinated fire protection and emergency services governance across the City of Gaylord and the five surrounding townships of Otsego County, Michigan. Through shared resources, joint planning, and fiscal oversight, the Authority ensures every community has access to professional, well-equipped fire and rescue services.

The Authority is governed by a board of directors representing each member entity. Regular public meetings are held to conduct the business of the Authority with full transparency and accountability to the citizens we serve.
